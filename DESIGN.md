# Design System: Crazy.

## Logo

Direction B, "Fuori riga": "Crazy" in Unbounded 700 converted to paths, everything aligned except the z, tilted 12° off the line, and a violet circle for the period. The symbol is "z." on an ink tile (favicon, app icon, social avatar). Letters take `currentColor`; the dot is `violet` (`--logo-dot` to swap it, white on a violet field). One component, `Logo.astro`, for nav and footer; in the nav a hover plays the short version (z wobble, dot hop). Source of truth for the paths: `src/data/logo.ts`. Rejected directions A and C live on the logo canvas, archive page.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Giorno"**

The agency in daylight. The same showreel as the night site (particle "Crazy.", stacked services, the client prism, the lab strip, the orb), set on the light a shop owner's own website lives in. The ground is a lilac paper (`bg`), panels are plain white, and ink is a violet-black, never pure black. Violet stops being a rare accent and becomes a field: whole surfaces are painted in it (the closing band, the quote summary, the "your site" prism face, the person monogram) and every primary action is a violet pill. Teal survives only inside the particles.

Structure stays quiet so motion can be loud. Containers are 22px white panels with an inset hairline; controls are pills; client work fills rounded media panels with no browser chrome. Depth is soft and tinted: shadows are a deep violet at low alpha, so cards lift off the paper without going grey. Unbounded (wide, geometric) carries names, headlines and figures; Geist carries reading and UI. Motion follows the scroll and is reversible; the one ambient loop is the orb's turning ring. Everything reads complete without WebGL and with reduced motion.

This world is the owner's pinned light exploration of v3. It refuses both the dark tech shell and the cream-plus-serif default; the dark v3 remains the live candidate in its own folder.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Lilac paper ground (`bg`) with a second paper step (`bg-2`) for the footer, values band and next-case band; white panels on top.
- Violet as whole fields and as the action colour, always carrying ink text (`on-violet`), never white.
- Violet as text on paper is always the deep `violet-ink`.
- Teal only inside the hero particles.
- Pills for anything you press or tag; 22px panels for containers and media; 14px inner panels.
- Soft violet-tinted shadows and inset hairlines; a faint multiply grain over everything.
- Motion follows the scroll (scrub), with a still, legible end state under reduced motion.

### Named Rules
**The Violet Field Rule.** Violet is applied as a whole surface or a whole control, never as a sliver: a band, a card, a face, a pill. No violet gradients, borders or decorative strokes on paper.

**The Ink On Violet Rule.** Text on violet is always `on-violet` ink; secondary text on violet is ink at 0.78 or more. White text on violet is off-system.

**The Violet Ink Rule.** Violet that has to be read on paper or white (links, focus, icons, prices, names) is `violet-ink`. Plain `violet` is a fill, not a text or line colour.

**The Teal Moves Rule.** Teal exists only inside the particles. A static teal button, heading, border or tag is off-system.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Unbounded Variable (with Arial Black, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif)

**Character:** Unbounded is wide, round-shouldered and loud; at 600/700 with tight negative tracking (-0.03em to -0.06em) it gives names and headlines the "crazy" voice. Geist is neutral and precise, so reading stays calm on the paper.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Unbounded speaks names, headlines and figures; Geist does everything you read or operate. Never set a paragraph in Unbounded or a section headline in Geist.

**The Off-Line Letter Rule.** Like the logo's z, a few chosen headlines carry one tilted letter (`tilt()` in `src/data/tilt.ts`, mark it as `[x]`): the hero h1 (the t of "dettaglio"/"detail", -12°), the about h1 (the z of "pazzi"/"crazy", -12°), the closing question mark (+16°) and the last 4 of the 404 (+14°). The letter enters upright and tips over with a small overshoot when the heading appears; with reduced motion it is simply tilted. Never more than one per heading, never on body copy or buttons.

**The Section Over Card Rule.** A section headline (`headline`) is always larger than the titles of the cards inside it (`service-card`).

**The No Label Rule.** There are no mono labels, no uppercase tracked kickers, no numbered section tags. A section starts with its headline; supporting lines (meta, kind, caption) sit below the name they describe.

## Layout

A single centred container (`container`, padded by `gutter`) on full-bleed paper. Sections breathe with `section` padding; the closing band and the services section use clamp(6rem, 12vw, 10rem), the prism, person and lab sections clamp(5rem, 10vw, 8rem), the FAQ clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem). Section heads are a flex row (headline left, one text link right) that wraps on small screens; the services head splits headline and lead into two columns from 900px.

- **Hero:** full small-viewport height (100svh). The particle word fills the upper area, centred over a faint violet wash; h1 and subline sit bottom-left, the primary pill and a text link bottom-right from 960px. Nothing else in the first viewport besides the nav.
- **Services stack:** three service cards; a plain column (gap 1.25rem) below 900px. From 900px each card is sticky at 6rem plus `stack-offset` times its index; text (0.9fr) beside the example (1.1fr), min-height min(36rem, 100vh minus 9rem).
- **Work (home):** text column (0.8fr) beside the prism stage (1.2fr) when the prism is on; otherwise one column with inline thumbnails.
- **Person:** photo or monogram (0.8fr, 4:5, max 26rem) beside the text (1.2fr) from 860px.
- **Lab strip:** a single-column grid of 16:10 cards on phones; one flex row from 900px at clamp(22rem, 32vw, 30rem) tall.
- **FAQ (home):** heading and link above, questions in two columns from 760px; from 1080px the heading column (0.7fr) sits beside the list (1.3fr).
- **Closing band:** headline and lead stacked above the orb; from 900px a two-column grid (1fr, auto) with the orb at the right.
- **Quote configurator:** form card plus a sticky 340px violet summary from 1000px; three equal step tracks; fields two-up from 700px.
- **Work page:** alternating media and text rows (1.35fr / 1fr from 900px), flipping side every other case.
- **Tap targets:** text links, nav controls and footer links are at least `tap-target` (2.75rem, 44px) tall.
- **Breakpoints in use:** 700, 760, 800, 860, 900, 960, 1000, 1080px. The prism also requires a viewport height of 620px or more.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid of tonal paper and soft tinted shadow. White panels sit on lilac paper and are outlined with inset hairlines; anything that floats (service cards, media panels, nav, photo frame) carries a low, wide shadow coloured with `shadow-tint`, never grey or black. A fixed film grain (SVG noise, 3.5% opacity, multiply) lies over the page and never intercepts input.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Soft drop** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -32px rgb(58 40 130 / 0.28), 0 10px 22px -14px rgb(58 40 130 / 0.16)`): service cards, work windows, services figures, case stage, mobile work thumbnails, gallery images, photo frame.
- **Incoming card** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line), 0 -18px 40px -14px rgb(58 40 130 / 0.22), <soft drop>`): every service card after the first, from 900px, so the arriving card reads as a layer over the one beneath even without animation.
- **Button lift** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 28px -16px rgb(75 59 176 / 0.55), inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.45)`): primary button only.
- **Nav float** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line), 0 10px 30px -18px rgb(58 40 130 / 0.25)`), turning to **Nav solid** (`inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line-2), 0 18px 40px -22px rgb(58 40 130 / 0.3)`) once the page leaves the top.
- **Orb core** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -20px rgb(22 19 31 / 0.6)`): the ink core on the violet band, where a tinted shadow would vanish.
- **Case stage under-glow** (`box-shadow: 0 60px 120px -60px rgb(157 141 241 / 0.22)`): added below the case-study stage only.
- **Prism floor**: a radial `rgb(58 40 130 / 0.25)` ellipse under the prism, fading out by 70%.

### Named Rules
**The Tinted Shadow Rule.** Shadows on paper are `shadow-tint` at 0.30 or less. Only the orb core, which sits on violet, uses an ink shadow.

**The Hairline Rule.** Never use `border` for structure on panels; use the inset hairline so radii and nesting stay exact. Real borders exist only on form inputs and the phone screenshot frame.

## Shapes

Three radii carry the system: pills (`pill`) for every button, tag, chip, choice pill and search bar; 22px (`lg`) for containers, service cards, lab cards, the quote summary, the photo frame and every large media panel (prism faces, work windows, case stage, services figures); 14px (`md`) for inner panels, choice boxes, inputs, service-card example screens, mobile work thumbnails and gallery images. SEO mock rows, the AI answer and the mobile lab caption plate use 12px (`inset`). The phone screenshot frame uses `device`; the scrollbar thumb `scrollbar`; focus outlines round to `focus`. The orb and its core are the only true circles (`circle`); burger bars round to `bar`.

### Named Rules
**The Full-Bleed Work Rule.** Client work fills its media panel edge to edge (object-fit cover, pinned to the top) with a `line-2` ring. No title bar, no window dots, no URL strip.

### Motion vocabulary
- **Easing:** `ease-out` cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) for entrances and state; `ease-in-out` cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1) for long moves and the orb pulse; `ease-drawer` cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1) for the lab strip.
- **Durations:** 160ms press, 200ms colour, 220 to 260ms icon and arrow nudges, 320ms quote step and burger, 420ms orb hover, 520 to 620ms reveal, 640ms strip.
- **Particles:** WebGL2 points sampled from the logo's own vector paths (`src/data/logo.ts`), violet to teal across the word with rare ink sparks. Intro (about 3.6s): the z assembles first and kicks the violet dot; the dot flies an anticlockwise, flattened elliptical orbit around "zy" (behind the letters on the far side, in front and larger near the "r") while the other letters assemble right to left, then lands after the y with a small squash-and-stretch hop (`src/scripts/logo-motion.ts`). The dot is an SVG circle on two layers, under and over the canvas. Afterwards the particles breathe, part around the mouse, and disperse as the hero scrolls away. Play (after the intro, not with reduced motion): a click or tap on the particles makes a small burst that settles back in about 2s; a click on the dot shoots it off with gravity, bouncing off the hero's edges with squash on impact, then it arcs home and lands with the hop. The static SVG logo shows until the canvas is lit, so no-WebGL and reduced motion see the finished logo.
- **Reveal:** first entry only; starts at 0.25 opacity, 14px down, 4px blur, never from invisible.
- **Work page:** each window straightens from rotateX(24deg) rotateY(plus or minus 10deg) scale 0.9 on a CSS view timeline (entry 0% to cover 45%), only where supported and motion is allowed.
- **Reduced motion:** transitions collapse, scroll scenes render their final frame, the service stack stays flat, the prism stays off, the orb is still, particles render formed and still.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use violet as whole fields (bands, cards, faces, the monogram) and as primary action pills, always with `on-violet` ink text.
- **Do** set violet text, links, icons and focus outlines in `violet-ink`.
- **Do** build containers as one 22px white `panel` with an inset hairline, inner panels at 14px on `panel-2`.
- **Do** tint every shadow on paper with `shadow-tint` and keep it soft and wide.
- **Do** make every button, tag and chip a pill; put the primary arrow in its own icon island.
- **Do** tie motion to scroll position (scrub) and give every scene a legible still end state for reduced motion and no-JS.
- **Do** show work full-bleed in a rounded `media-well` panel and label every concept as a concept.
- **Do** keep prices inside the quote configurator only.
- **Do** give every link and control a tap target of at least 2.75rem (44px).
- **Do** gate hover effects behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` and keep press feedback at scale 0.97 (0.98 on large choice boxes).

### Don't:
- **Don't** put white text on violet, or secondary ink on violet below 0.78.
- **Don't** use plain `violet` for text, thin lines or focus rings on paper or white; it reads at about 2.5:1.
- **Don't** use teal outside the particles.
- **Don't** use gradient text, glows, neon edges, text-shadow or violet gradients.
- **Don't** wrap work in a browser-window frame (title bar, dots, URL strip).
- **Don't** add eyebrows, kickers, mono uppercase labels or numbered section tags such as "(01) SERVIZI".
- **Don't** add a marquee strip of service keywords or a "Thiene to worldwide" tag in the hero.
- **Don't** add a full-screen curtain or page-transition overlay, a custom cursor, or smooth-scroll hijacking.
- **Don't** use particles outside the home hero or make any scroll scene depend on them.
- **Don't** drift into the cream-plus-serif default or bring back the v2 shop-window world (amber light, shop signs, street scenery).
- **Don't** use pure black for ink or shadows; ink is `ink` and shadows are `shadow-tint`.
