# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of small local businesses (gelaterie, fioristi, associazioni, negozi, professionisti), mostly non-technical, who want a website that sets them apart and brings customers. They arrive comparing options and need to understand quickly what they get and what it costs.
Secondary: more structured brands and companies looking for an author-quality website.

## Product Purpose

Crazy. is a web agency based in Thiene (VI), working with clients anywhere. The site exists to win quote requests: a visitor should see the quality of the work, trust it, and start the quote configurator.

## Positioning

Made-to-measure websites crafted down to the detail ("siti su misura, curati in ogni dettaglio"; no off-the-shelf templates; the user rejected the earlier "prezzi da bottega" slogan, prices stay transparent in the listino but are not the headline), delivered fast, built to be found on Google and by AI assistants, with a direct relationship: you talk to the people who build the site (the agency is Marco and Giorgia).

## Operating Context

Visitors come from local word of mouth, Google and AI assistants, often on phones. The decisive moment is the quote configurator: landing page from 400 €, multi-page site from 700 €, site + e-commerce from 1,200 € (VAT excluded).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: showcase websites, e-commerce, SEO and AI SEO.
- Languages: Italian (primary) and English.
- Stack: Astro (static), deploy on any static host.
- Particles (WebGL) only in the home hero, independent from the rest of the page; scrollytelling must not depend on them.
- Must stay fast: no heavy intro or page-transition overlay on every page.
- Undecided: final domain, contact email/WhatsApp, VAT number, what each price package includes.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work, don't describe it: real client sites lead, concepts are labelled.
2. Clear prices, plain words: no jargon, no slogans.
3. Fast is part of the craft: every effect must earn its weight.
4. Local first, open to the world, without saying it in taglines.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast; full content without WebGL or with reduced motion.
